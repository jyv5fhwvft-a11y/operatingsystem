GRUB reads grub.cfg, displays the boot menu, and loads vmlinuz and initrd.
BIOS runs POST, finds the boot device, and loads the 512-byte MBR to 0x7C00.
GRUB switches the CPU to Protected Mode and jumps to the Linux kernel.
core.img uses its built-in filesystem drivers to access the /boot partition.
GRUB boot.img executes and loads core.img from the post-MBR gap.
